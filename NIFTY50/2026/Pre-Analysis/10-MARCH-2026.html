After a volatile session, the market managed to reclaim 24,000 and close above it. That late push higher suggests buyers stepped in toward the end of the day.

Now the real question for tomorrow is simple: Can the market hold above 24,000, or was this just a temporary squeeze?

Most of tomorrow's action will likely revolve around how price behaves around that level.
