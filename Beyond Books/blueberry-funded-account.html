Section 06 The Equity Reset Trap: The Rule That Ends Most Accounts

Most account terminations are not caused by bad strategy. They are caused by a misunderstanding of how the daily loss limit actually works — specifically how it behaves when you hold positions overnight.

The daily loss limit is equity-based, not balance-based. It resets every day at server midnight and is calculated against whatever your equity was at the start of that day — not your original $5,000.

Case Study — The Overnight Reset Trap
How a Profitable Account Gets Terminated at Breakeven
Day 1 ends: open position pushes equity to $5,200
Server resets at midnight — new day baseline = $5,200
Daily loss limit for the new day = $200 from $5,200
Market retraces — equity falls back to $5,000
System registers a $200 loss for the day → Account Terminated
Your account balance is $5,000 — exactly where you started. But the account is gone because the daily loss was calculated from $5,200, not $5,000. The rule governs equity movement within the calendar day, not profit or loss from your original entry price.

The straightforward mitigation is to close all positions before the daily server reset. Operate intraday. This keeps your baseline clean at the start of every new session and eliminates the possibility of an involuntary breach on a trade that is technically profitable from your original entry.

Section 07 MT5 Setup: The Technical Steps That Cannot Be Skipped

After purchase, your dashboard will display MT5 credentials. The server configuration step is where most traders make errors — usually by selecting the wrong server from the search results or mistyping case-sensitive credentials.

MT5 Connection Protocol — Three Steps
①
Retrieve Credentials from Dashboard

Navigate to Active Accounts → Credentials. Copy your Login ID and Password directly — do not retype them. Credentials are case-sensitive and a single character error is the most common cause of login failure.

②
Select the Correct Server

In MT5, go to New Account and search for Blueberry Markets SVG LLC or Blueberry Markets SVG Live. If the server does not appear immediately, type the full name manually. Connecting to any other server will result in authentication failure.

③
Validate with a Minimum-Size Trade

Before executing your strategy, open a 0.01 lot position on a high-liquidity instrument — XAUUSD or BTCUSD. This confirms server connectivity and execution speed without risking your daily loss buffer.
