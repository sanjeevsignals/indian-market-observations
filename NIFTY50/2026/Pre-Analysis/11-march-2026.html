By Sanjeev Singh  ·  Sanjeev Signals  ·  Independent F&O Researcher
Market Context

The recent daily candle shows a sharp recovery from the lower support area near 23,697, followed by immediate rejection around 24,300. The structure is telling a clear story.

1 Buyers were active at the lower liquidity zone near 23,697.
2 Sellers are still defending the higher supply area around 24,300.

In simple terms, this bounce appears more like short covering inside a broader bearish structure — not a confirmed trend reversal. Because of that, tomorrow should be approached with a level-driven framework, not prediction.

Market Psychology

The current structure reflects a classic situation. Understanding this distinction prevents reactive trading:

What the Bounce Looks Like
Strong recovery from lower support. A visually convincing move higher.
What the Bounce Actually Is
Likely short covering — not fresh buying inside a bearish trend.
