Trading Framework
01
First 15–30 Min

Opening Range Control

Do nothing immediately at open. Let the first range form. A large gap-down typically produces three distinct phases before a tradeable structure emerges.

Panic flush — initial capitulation selling
Short covering spike — squeeze of weak shorts
False breakdown — head-fake below key support

Define Opening High & Opening Low. Trade only on breakdown continuation below the opening low, or a structured pullback to VWAP / breakdown level rejection. No impulsive entry.

02
Trend Continuation

Pullback-to-Structure Selling

Gap-down days favour the sell-side — but only under strict conditions. Confirm the environment before committing.

Price stays below VWAP
Lower highs keep forming on every rally
No aggressive reclaim of previous day's close
📐 Sell pullbacks. Never chase red candles. Crash opens reward patience, not speed. The move has already happened — wait for price to come to you.
03
Gap Fill Scenario

Reversal Trap Awareness

Large gap-down days can rotate violently. Be alert for two failure modes that trap directional traders.

Short-covering rally — bears caught overextended
Gap-fill attempt — price reclaims prior session range

If price reclaims the opening range high with strength, bias shifts neutral — not bearish. Do not fight the reclaim.

🧭 Levels decide → Bias follows → Execution. The market doesn't owe you a trend. Respect what price is actually doing.
04
Capital Preservation

What NOT To Do

Crash days seduce traders with false certainty. The following behaviours destroy accounts on volatile days — eliminate them entirely.

🚫Bottom calling — catching falling knives
🚫Blind option buying at the open bell
🚫Emotional revenge trading after a loss
🚫Over-leveraging due to "high conviction"

"Crash days create opportunity — but only for traders who respect structure over emotion."

— Professional Playbook Rule Zero
